# IDD v1.8 — Grilling interview and retro

Plugin 1.8.0. Framework v1.8.

## What changed

- **Grilling interview.** `interview` now asks in rounds, with a numbered
  recommended answer for every question; "yes" accepts it. It looks facts up in
  the project instead of asking, challenges vague or conflicting vocabulary,
  and stops only when nothing is left open and the stakeholder confirms.
- **Grilling in outcome authoring.** define-intentions, define-expectations and
  define-outcomes use the same discipline; edge cases are proposed as
  recommended answers.
- **Product records.** Products gain optional `glossary`, `decisions` and
  `open_questions`. Technical questions a PM can't answer are recorded as
  `needs: tech-lead`; write-spec resolves relevant ones or stops, and gap-check
  blocks a Spec while one is open.
- **Product brief.** A readable `docs/products/<stem>.md` is rendered from the
  YAML after every save. The YAML stays canonical; nobody edits the brief.
- **Retro stage.** New `retro` workflow (`/idd-framework:retro`, skill
  `idd-retro`) runs after validation and before archive. It proposes
  evidence-traced fixes and applies only approved ones. Reports live at
  `docs/reviews/SPEC-<id>-retro.md`; their `lessons` are kept in the ledger.
- **Nudges.** review-spec recommends a retro when gaps or failures were
  recorded; archive warns (`retro-missing`) without blocking.
- **Model tiers.** product-interviewer, intention-author and expectation-author
  move from Haiku to Sonnet.

## Inventory

Sixteen stages, seventeen portable skills (including the router), sixteen
Claude commands, fourteen role agents.

## Migration

None required. New Product fields and the ledger `lessons` field are
optional; existing artifacts stay valid. Re-running the interview on an
existing Product grills only its gaps and adds a brief.

## Not yet verified

Live host evaluations (Codex and Claude) of the new interview, authoring and
retro behaviour have not been run. Structural checks and oracle tests pass.
